# About these icons

The line icons on Afterglow's own buttons and menus (add, the row menu, the arrows and
so on) are Google's **Material Symbols**, Outlined style, 24 px, from
https://github.com/google/material-design-icons (`symbols/web/<name>/materialsymbolsoutlined/`),
taken unchanged. They are licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**; its text is in
`LICENSE` beside them. Afterglow draws them in the text colour of the window's theme
(`gui/symbols.py`).

Files: add, arrow_forward, chevron_left, chevron_right, content_copy, delete,
drag_indicator, edit, history, menu, more_horiz, search.
