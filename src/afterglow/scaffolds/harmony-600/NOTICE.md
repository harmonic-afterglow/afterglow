# About this tree

The Harmony 600's look: the pictures here were **extracted from a real Harmony 600
configuration**. They are Logitech-authored artwork, **not covered by this project's
GPL-3.0 licence**, and they are here for the same reason `../harmony-650/`'s are: the
remote cannot be driven without them, and nothing else looks right on it.

This is not a whole scaffold. A Harmony 600 runs the Harmony 650's firmware program and
configuration format, so the 650 backend builds it from `../harmony-650/` - its wiring,
firmware screens and fonts - and takes from here only what is the 600's own (`look.json`):
these grey pictures and the light levels in section 15's tail.

Only artwork and platform state were taken. Nothing that identifies a remote or its owner -
no device names, activities, favourites or account data - was read from the donor
configuration, and none of it appears here.
